Budget App

Privacy Policy

This is a student budget management application created for coursework and personal learning.

Budget records, language preference, and cookie consent are stored only in your browser localStorage.

The app does not use server-side tracking, accounts, analytics, or databases, and it does not upload your budget data.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings.

隐私政策

这是一个用于课程作业和个人学习的学生预算管理应用。

预算记录、语言偏好和 Cookie 同意状态只存储在你的浏览器 localStorage 中。

本应用不使用服务器端跟踪、账户、分析工具或数据库，也不会上传你的预算数据。

你可以随时通过浏览器设置清除站点数据。
